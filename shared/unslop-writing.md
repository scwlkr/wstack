# Writing

Creation + editing → specific, useful prose; preserve facts, qualifications, quotations and intended voice.
- Empty intro/praise/ending → delete: “Great question”, “In today's fast-paced world”, “The future looks bright.”
- Inflated/vague claims → supplied capability: “pivotal export improvement” → “Exports include archived orders.”
- “Seamlessly streamline workflow” → “Assign leads; see who needs follow-up.” Never invent specificity, authority or experience.
- “Not just X; Y” → actual distinction/capability; retain meaningful contrasts.
- Noun piles/jargon → direct verbs: “implementation of validation” → “validate.” Keep precise domain terms.
- One entity → one term; no synonym cycling (“workspace/hub/portal”).
- Stacked hedges → preserve confidence with fewer words: “could potentially” → “may.”
- Lists/rhythm follow content; no filler third item or identical paragraph template.
- Repeated bold labels/emoji/em dashes/transitions → remove when they interrupt; retain useful scan aids.
- Review-only → findings; otherwise deliver revised artifact. No edits solely to look different.
- Patterns need context, not banned words; useful character/formality stays.
